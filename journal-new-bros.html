Make Sense
Hackney Brocals

3rd November 2020

We gathered with the Hackney Brocals at U7 Lounge for an afternoon of making sketchbooks and drawing to sound.

This pre-lockdown session took place at U7 Lounge on Regent's Canal in Haggerston, London. The participants were members of Hackney Brocals, a group of men aged 50 and over who combat loneliness ("broneliness") by engaging in activities together.

Each participant created a simple sketchbook by folding and cutting paper. Then, a series of sounds, some musical and some more abstract, were played to inspire the participants to draw. Each sound prompted them to decorate a different page in their sketchbook. At the end of the session, we unfolded the sketchbooks to reveal a large sheet displaying the collection of everyone's drawings.

Because sounds don't have a defined visual form, this process is liberating for those who don't often draw. It allows participants to express themselves in a truly individual way. For those experiencing dementia, music and sound can be especially stimulating, evoking memories and helping to explore new avenues of expression through rhythm.
